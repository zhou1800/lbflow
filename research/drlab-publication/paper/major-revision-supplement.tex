\subsection{Continuous manufactured sources}
Use physical height $H_p=16$, periodic length $L=128$ and
\begin{equation}
 \eta(y,t)=.25\cos(\pi y/H_p)\cos(.05t)+.0002t,\qquad
 d=L/4-|\operatorname{wrap}(x-L/2-\eta;L)|.
\end{equation}
Near the interface $\varphi_*=[1+\tanh(.62d)]/2$.
For $|d|>23$, a quintic polynomial joins this logistic tail to an exact pure
plateau at $|d|=27$. Value and its first two derivatives match at 23 and
vanish at 27; the upper tail is complementary. This $C^2$ construction
removes the absolute-distance cusp in the pure bulk and changes only
logistic tails below $5\times10^{-13}$.
It is an analytic target, not numerical clipping.

For each prescribed hydraulic slip $\lambda$ let
\begin{align}
 \psi_*&=4\times10^{-8} A(y)\sin(2\pi x/L),\\
 A(y)&=y^2(H_p-y)^2+c_\lambda y(H_p-y),\qquad
 c_\lambda=\frac{2\lambda H_p^2}{H_p+2\lambda},\\
 \bm u_*&=(\partial_y\psi_*,-\partial_x\psi_*),\\
 p_*&=10^{-5}\cos(2\pi x/L)\cos(\pi y/H_p).
\end{align}
$A=0$ at the walls and its derivatives satisfy Navier slip.
The analytically differentiated sources are
\begin{align}
 \bm F_{\mathrm{ext},*}
 &=-\nu\Delta\bm u_*+\nabla p_*-\sigma\kappa_*\nabla\varphi_*,\\
 S_*&=\partial_t\varphi_*+\bm u_*\cdot\nabla\varphi_*
 -D\nabla\cdot[\nabla\varphi_*-2b\varphi_*(1-\varphi_*)\bm n_*].
\end{align}
The actual numerical capillary force is still evaluated from the evolving
field, so its errors are not canceled by discretely manufactured forcing.
Continuous scalar derivatives likewise do not call the face-flux routine.

At $t=16$, phase error is normalized by $\|\varphi_*\|_2$; velocity error
uses both staggered components with denominator $\|\bm u_*\|_2$.
The additional phase-displacement error divides
$\|\varphi-\varphi_*\|_2$ by the norm of the analytic normal-profile
derivative. At slip 1 these displacement errors are
$3.22527\times10^{-5}$, $7.76476\times10^{-6}$ and
$1.92697\times10^{-6}$ in length units.
At slip 4 they are $4.12151\times10^{-5}$,
$1.00931\times10^{-5}$ and $2.51358\times10^{-6}$.
Time stepping uses $\Delta t=.125h^2$, with a separate
$\Delta t=.0625h^2$ H32/slip1 calculation.
The mass check integrates the actual continuous source samples with the
same temporal quadrature and reports its budget residual.
Analytic derivative checks are independently retained.

Reconstruction clips only arguments of logarithms at $10^{-12}$;
the evolving $\varphi$ is not clipped.
That regularization is active in the exact pure manufactured plateaus,
unlike the ordinary primary front comparisons.
The tiny retained pure-tail overshoots do not establish a monotonicity or
boundedness property. The earlier non-$C^2$ pilot is retained separately,
outside the seven canonical tests.

\subsection{Intervention and reference refinement}
The selective reflected Laplacian has zero face flux at both walls.
Its squared form has the continuum conditions
$\varphi_y=\varphi_{yyy}=0$ in the corresponding smooth reflection limit.
These are numerical regularization choices; they do not supply a microscopic
moving-contact-line boundary law. In a weighted split the exterior Laplacian
retains zero net flux. Nonnegative wall and bulk mobilities give
$\langle\varphi,-K L_y[wL_y\varphi]\rangle
=-K\langle L_y\varphi,wL_y\varphi\rangle\le0$.
The independent cosine, partition, mass and quadratic-work controls are
stored as matrix/operator calculations, not full shape trajectories.

The reference adds H64/H96 at fixed sampled crossing width and a separate
H48/H64/H96 sequence at fixed continuum
$b=2\operatorname{atanh}(.9)/W_{0.9}$.
The latter uses the corresponding sampled width at each spacing rather
than changing $b$ to match an interpolated crossing.
No contact-line asymptotic order is assigned to either sequence.
The optional DST-I/rank-two inversion agrees with the original dense
Stokes matrix; dense remains the default for earlier reanalysis.

The low-tension H16/H32 untreated FV runs and H32 unit-strength FV run
cover physical time 16000. The original shorter runs are kept.
The H32 production continuation imports and hashes its exact outgoing
native donor. The chosen longest-duration means are explicitly recorded
in each reduced-reference comparison.
Mean, centroid and output-interval changes remain separate in the data.

\subsection{Reduced model construction and shape observable}
At each grid, take the analytic logistic bridge independently matched to
$W_{0.9}=4.765235$. Its signed derivative $p'$ is positive at the ascending
interface and negative at the descending one.
Centered perturbations $\pm\epsilon[-p'\eta]$ construct each column of the
capillary/Stokes response. The conservative ascending-half phase-rate
projection is $-h\sum_x\dot\varphi/(h\sum_xp')$.
The same projection of the body-force response constructs the drive.
No full-front measurement is read by
\texttt{phase\_mechanism\_controls.py}.

The bordered linear system fixes $\sum_y\eta=0$ and gives both translation
speed and deformation. A separately calculated left null vector verifies
that speed; velocity and shape comparison data do not alter the matrix.
The full mean fluid velocity is computed from the resulting Stokes field,
rather than substituted for the projected phase speed.
A small matrix-row correction enforces the analytic rigid-translation
identity only after checking its finite-difference residue.
The receipt retains the residue, linear-system error, null-vector error,
matrix spectrum and differentiation-amplitude repeat.

The measured row displacement is
$z(y)=-h\sum_{x\in\mathrm{ascending\ half}}\varphi(x,y)$ with its row mean
subtracted. Its reduced prediction is $(h\sum_xp')\eta(y)$.
No contour position, shape amplitude or speed is fitted.
The largest full-FV volume-shape reduction error is
\MajorFVShapePercent\%, despite the much smaller mean-speed errors.
This is an explicit limitation of the retained-normal-profile approximation.

\subsection{Reanalysis and exclusions}
\paragraph{Additional mode-range sensitivity.}
The resistance response involves modes above the three full-solver wave
controls. In a separate held-profile matrix diagnostic, retaining the
fourth-order intervention only for $m\leq3$ changes the H48/slip1 mean speed
by $-10.4587$\% relative to the all-mode intervention.
We therefore compare all reflected modes $m=1,\ldots,H-1$ with the exact
rest-map translation eigenvalues. The corresponding semidiscrete rates
$(1-\mu_m)/h^2$ are distinct from the discrete-time log rates used for the
full wave trajectories. Replacing the fourth-order diagonal rates by these
exact semidiscrete rates, while retaining the same capillary/Stokes response,
changes the predicted mean speed by 0.11663\%, 0.03988\% and 0.01663\%
at H16/H32/H48 and slip1, and by 0.00545\% at H48/slip4.
The selected translation overlaps exceed 0.9979 in all these modes.
Over the even modes excited by the drive, the formal reflected fourth-order
rates differ from the exact semidiscrete eigenrates by at most 2.020\%,
0.877\% and 0.435\%, respectively.
The archived \texttt{modal\_response\_check.py} records complete spectra,
mode truncations and an instantaneous-projection alternative.
These are additional reduced-matrix checks, not new full LBM/FV trajectories
or a uniform modified equation for moving interfaces.

\texttt{data/major-revision.json} is regenerated from frozen snapshots,
receipts and source identities. The collector verifies native inputs,
geometry, executables, seed profiles and outgoing donors; it recomputes
centroids, wave fits, manufactured errors and full hydraulic fields.
The predictive model is recalculated in a separate process from its frozen
source, with no front data input. Old receipts are relocated in memory;
their original bytes are preserved.
The raw manifest and relocated reanalysis checks identify the exact evidence
inventory and canonical measurement digest.

Three reference CLI pilots failed before calculation because a width option
was unavailable in their frozen version. Their logs are preserved.
A reduced-model run calculated its summary but failed during final console
printing; it is retained outside the canonical prediction set and rerun
with the same numerical formulas. The pre-$C^2$ manufacture and preliminary
analysis products are similarly excluded from publication counts.
Failures are not silently converted into successful controls.
Older primitive FV, same-node slip and superseded-kernel failures remain
in the preserved pre-revision archive.
