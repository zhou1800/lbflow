\section{Fixed-width front resistance and reciprocal work}
The new campaign retains the pressure-core executable recorded in the
preceding section. No collision, force, wetting or phase-transfer kernel
changes. Projection recoloring, legacy MRT, equal tau1 and theta60 are used.
Each lattice length unit is $d=16/H$ in H16 base units, and its time unit
is $d^2$. Surface tension is $0.005d$ in lattice units. Thus
$U_b=U_l/d$, $p_b=p_l/d^2$, $M_b=M_l d$ and $R_b=R_l/d$.
Leading axial scalar diffusion and viscosity both remain $1/6$ in base units.
This is a matched-width grid sequence with a finite width ratio
$W_{0.9}/H=0.29782719$, not a sharp-interface limit or a verified
constant physical slip length.

For the descending planar recurrence, let $u=\varphi_j$, $v=\varphi_{j+1}$
and $C=(1-\beta)u+\beta u^2$. The stable quadratic branch is
\begin{equation}
 v=\frac{2C}{1+\beta+\sqrt{(1+\beta)^2-4\beta C}}.
\end{equation}
Symmetry across the midpoint gives the first high-side fraction
$u=[1+(\sqrt{1+\beta^2}-1)/\beta]/2$ and the first low-side fraction
$1-u$ at normal distance $1/2$. Linear interpolation to fraction $0.05$
gives half the 0.9-color width. Bisection selects beta solely from this
profile. Full periodic controls of lengths 128/256/384 and height four,
run for 8000 steps, agree with independently time-marched scalar profiles
within $4.87\times10^{-12}$. Full measured widths are
4.76523502/9.53047004/14.29570506. The gradient cutoff remains $10^{-9}$;
dilute tails and crossing interpolation are retained.

Closed $4H$ donors run for 32000/128000/288000 steps.
Their final numerical pressure supplies each front's pressure center;
the prescribed pressure pairs are selected before the front calculation.
Single-front runs use 8000/32000/72000 steps, followed by exact outgoing
continuations to 16000/64000/144000. Late volume speeds use the latter
half of each receipt's output interval. At H16 the pressure halfwidths are
0.01/0.02/0.04 times $p_Y$ for both lengths; H32/H48 use 0.02.
The separate centerline and first-row crossings remain in the JSON
histories. They involve subgrid interpolation and are not substituted for
volume transport or presented as resolved wall-contact positions.
Pure channels use a pressure difference of magnitude $10^{-5}$ in both
directions, with their own relaxation durations. Measured central gradients,
midpoint profiles and exact kinetic budgets check these controls.

The interfacial excess resistance in the table is in each lattice's native
units. For cross-grid comparison it must be divided by $d$, yielding
approximately 0.06231, 0.11306 and 0.14269 at $L=4H$.
Near equality of native H16 long/short excess resistance is a localization
test at one grid; it does not establish general length independence at
other widths or wetting angles. The maximum new pressure-budget residual
relative to initial interior mass is $\FrontMaxBudget$.

The reciprocal auxiliary velocity is half the signed difference of the
two independently measured pure-channel fields. Its flux is the sum of
axial velocity across the middle section. The audited capillary force uses
the actual scalar gradient, ghost continuation, curvature and cutoff.
Outgoing color/density match the incoming physical macrostate at the same
time because collision and recoloring preserve local phase mass.
Both adjacent states are checked explicitly before work is calculated;
no audit force is substituted into the simulation.
The force-weighted pressure is the sum of actual force times auxiliary
velocity over fluid nodes, with zero force on PB and solid nodes, divided
by the auxiliary section flux. Spatial sums in both directions and all
audit/checkpoint hashes are recorded for independent reanalysis.

For common constant viscosity, incompressible Stokes stresses give
$\int\nabla\bm u_a:\bm\sigma\,dV=
\int\nabla\bm u:\bm\sigma_a\,dV$.
Integration of the two momentum balances then gives the reciprocal work
relation in the main text. Stationary wall velocities eliminate their
boundary work; pressure faces give the pressure--flux products.
The actual weakly compressible LBM pressure boundary and its discrete
stress do not satisfy all of these continuum assumptions exactly.
Absolute predicted mean speeds therefore retain residuals up to
$\FrontMaxForceResidual$, particularly material near the zero drift.
The incremental resistance comparison cancels most of that offset; it
is checked at every signed rate pair and is not a new exact LBM identity.

The independent scalar coefficient follows the known conservative
Allen--Cahn behavior of color-gradient recoloring \cite{subhedar2020}.
It is not the chemical-potential mobility in the Cahn--Hilliard
contact-line scaling of Kusumaatmaja et al. \cite{kusumaatmaja2016}.
An independent-mobility color-gradient model exists \cite{subhedar2022},
but is not implemented or validated by this campaign. None of these
citations makes the observed excess resistance a calibrated physical
contact-line coefficient. Raw currents, signed pressure error and
grid dependence remain in \texttt{data/front-mobility.json}.
