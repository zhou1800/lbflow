\section*{Program summary}
\noindent\textbf{Program title:} LBFlow.\\
\textbf{CPC Library link to program files:} to be assigned on actual program
archive deposition; no archive DOI is claimed in this research draft.\\
\textbf{Developer's repository link:} \url{https://github.com/zhou1800/lbflow}.\\
\textbf{Licensing provisions:} MIT for code; CC BY 4.0 for the attributed
Bentheimer image derivatives. The preserved cJSON source retains its notices.\\
\textbf{Programming language:} C++11; Python 3.10 or later for reproduction.\\
\textbf{External routines/libraries:} MPI; CMake 3.16 or later for building;
NumPy for verification, SciPy for image/reference analysis, Matplotlib for figures.\\
\textbf{Tested operating system:} Linux, x86-64, double precision.\\
\textbf{Nature of problem:} Pore-scale single-phase and immiscible two-phase
flow on segmented voxel images, with independently checked force, viscosity,
capillary and phase-flux conventions.\\
\textbf{Solution method:} D2Q9/D3Q19 lattice Boltzmann streaming, SRT or
raw-moment MRT, Guo forcing, color-gradient perturbation and recoloring,
geometrical wetting, halfway solid-link bounce-back and MPI domain exchange.\\
Optional local profile continuation and curvature surface forcing are
experimental controls with a separately recorded numerical scope.\\
\textbf{Restrictions:} Quantitative evidence is bounded to equal reference
densities, standard pressure weights, low speed and the documented cases.
Curved-wall wetting accuracy, steady rock relative permeability, high density
ratio, moving contact-line laws and GPU execution are not established.
The executable runs one configuration per MPI job.\\
\textbf{Running time and memory:} measured workload, repetitions, numerical
and I/O times, per-rank peak RSS and hardware are supplied in the performance
section and supplementary table; they are workload dependent.\\
\textbf{Supplementary material:} Operator derivations, analytic controls,
recorded numerical summaries, licensed small voxel inputs, preparation and
reproduction scripts, run-identity metadata and figure-generation code.
