Dear Editor,

Please consider "Discrete interface kinetics and capillary resistance in pore-scale lattice Boltzmann simulations with LBFlow" for the Computer Programs in Physics category of Computer Physics Communications.

The manuscript asks which numerical interface properties must be controlled before pore-scale two-phase transport can be interpreted physically. Its main contribution is a quantitative diagnosis of discrete phase kinetics. An exact zero-flow projection-stencil spectrum, including unit-normal variation, predicts shape-perturbation decay in fourteen complete LBM runs within 0.00074%, with a second wavelength checked without refitting. The grid and wavelength dependence is consistent with numerical fourth-order shape relaxation even though the leading signed-distance equilibrium phase flux vanishes.

A neutral displacement experiment prescribes the same hydraulic slip, viscosity and measured width for LBM and an independently verified Stokes/finite-volume reference. Their finest-grid mean-flow discrepancy remains 13.72%. A deliberately shared-phase ablation reduces it to 0.290%, while the independent Stokes prediction using actual production capillary force differs by 0.0619%. These controls identify a kinetic contribution; shared-operator agreement is explicitly distinguished from independent physical contact-line validation. No converged dynamic law is claimed.

Complementary pressure-driven tests localize excess resistance and recover its incremental response from capillary-force work. Diffuse-viscosity and conservative wall-transfer controls, an attributed public Bentheimer image and independent single-phase LBPM comparison support the application. Nonneutral currents, negative grid results and transient rock limits are retained.

The MIT-licensed C++/MPI program includes reconstruction scripts, source-kernel identities, raw-evidence hashes, operator derivations and MPI/restart checks. Established conservative Allen-Cahn mappings, signed-distance reconstruction, slip controls and related recent literature are acknowledged. AI-assisted work is disclosed.

Public software access, an immutable program-archive citation, author review and final submission attestations must be finalized before submission. This draft does not attest exclusive submission or publication rights on behalf of the author.
